# Typefill

Type a short code and get your full text, anywhere in your browser. Free, with no limits, and what you type never leaves your browser.

Typefill works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

- [Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/typefill/ljiilefogipbjnkofncdmgfbfmjfppon) (Edge and Brave install it from the Chrome Web Store too)
- [Add to Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/typefill-free-text-expander/)

## What it does

- **Shortcuts.** Type `/thanks` and it turns into your whole reply.
- **Fill-in boxes.** Text boxes, dropdowns, date pickers and parts you can tick on or off, all filled in a small box before the text goes in.
- **Dates, clipboard and caret.** Today's date in any format, what you last copied, and where the caret lands afterwards.
- **Formatting.** Bold, italic, links, lists and pictures.
- **Search.** Press Ctrl+Shift+Space to find a snippet by name.
- **Folders, import and export.** Bring your snippets from Text Blaze or a spreadsheet.
- **Sync.** Your snippets follow you through your own browser account. They're never stored on a server of ours.

It works in Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn, Slack, WhatsApp Web, Teams, Google Docs and Google Sheets, and in the editors most sites are built on.

## Typefill Pro

Typefill Pro adds if blocks, formulas, text from the page you're on, Google Sheets data and key steps that fill a whole form. It's a separate, paid part and its code isn't in this repository. The free version here works fully on its own.

## Privacy

Typefill never sends what you type anywhere. Your snippets stay in your browser and in your browser's own sync. With Pro, the only thing sent is your licence key and email, to check the licence.
